Evolution in the endovascular management of lower limb arteriopathy
Arteriopathy of the lower limbs is a frequent pathology.
In the face of persistent invalidating claudication or critical ischemia, surgical or endovascular revascularization treatment is necessary.
The aim of this article is to review the evolution of the endovascular therapeutic arsenal of the femoropopliteal segment, to present the new endovascular devices available and to give current recommendations for post-interventional antithrombotic treatment.
