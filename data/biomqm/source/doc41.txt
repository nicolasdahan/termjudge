Therapeutic approaches for proximal humerus fractures
Proximal humerus fracture (PHF) is the 3rd most common fracture over age 65, and its incidence increases with age.
The combination of an aging population and a high incidence in geriatric patients constitute a serious potential public health concern.
The treatment of PHF is debated.
Recent publications report similar results of conservative versus surgical treatment in several types of fractures.
Therapeutic decision based on a multiparametric analysis is recommended, there is yet no consensus on the parameters to be analyzed.
A recent study suggests taking into consideration individual parameters in deciding the therapeutic strategy and provides a pragmatic treatment algorithm.
We propose here a simplified version of this algorithm, guiding therapeutic decision between conservative treatment, osteosynthesis and arthroplasty.
