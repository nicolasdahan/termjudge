Pediatric chronic pain : interprofessional and integrative care
Pediatric chronic pain is an increasingly recognized condition in children and adolescents.
Current treatment is based on the bio-psycho-social model of chronic pain: an interprofessional and integrative team will help the young patient and his environment, in order to find a balance between physical, psychological and social impacts.
This article focuses on different treatment approaches for pediatric chronic pain in the outpatient and inpatient setting.
