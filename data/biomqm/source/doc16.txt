Percutaneous nephrolithotomy (PNL) in the treatment of nephrolithiasis: the CHUV experience
Percutaneous nephrolithotomy is the intervention of choice for intrarenal stones of > 2 cm.
As such, it is an essential treatment modality in the armamentarium of endourological centers.
Its miniaturization has allowed a diversification of methods, a lower morbidity and a widening of its indications.
We describe in the present article the different existing methods and present the first results of our cohort.
