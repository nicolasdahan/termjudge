Neural machine translation relies on a neural network to translate sentences.
The decoder generates the target sentence one token at a time.
An attention mechanism lets the decoder focus on relevant source words.
With attention, long sentences are translated more accurately.
Beam search keeps the most promising hypotheses during decoding.
Machine translation systems trained this way outperform earlier approaches.
MT quality is usually measured against reference translations.
In this paper, we evaluate neural networks of different sizes.
